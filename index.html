About Our Association

The Non-Academic Staff Association (NASA) of the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay) is dedicated to supporting, representing, and enriching the professional ecosystem for our vital non-academic workforce.

Established in 1974, NASA promotes employee welfare, mutual respect, skill advancement, and a cohesive operating environment aligned with the excellence of the institute administration.

Our Six Pillars of Purpose

1. The Pillar of Well-being

Creating an environment where every staff member feels healthy, safe, and supported through wellness initiatives and caring programs.

2. The Pillar of Togetherness

Bringing people closer through cultural festivals, open forums, and recreational activities that strengthen our vibrant community.

3. The Pillar of Voice & Visibility

Providing a respected platform where every staff member's idea, concern, and aspiration finds space and response.

4. The Pillar of Recognition

Celebrating every achievement, big or small, and honouring the dedicated contributions of our staff.

5. The Pillar of Growth

Opening doors to learning and skill-building opportunities, empowering staff members to grow personally and professionally.

6. The Pillar of Unity & Inclusivity

Building a culture of equality and unity so every member feels they truly belong to the NASA family.

Vision of NASA

To nurture a strong sense of belonging and unity among the staff members of IIT Bombay by promoting well-being, mutual respect, and active participation in community life.

Mission of NASA

To build an inclusive, supportive, and vibrant community where every staff member feels valued, connected, and motivated to contribute towards the shared progress of IIT Bombay, while nurturing a positive and collaborative environment.
